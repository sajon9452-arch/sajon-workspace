# Global Project Rules & Architectural Directives

## 1. Calendar Monthly Detail View
- **Pure Image Banners (No Text Overlay)**: For every single month of the year (January through December), the designated banner card area at the top of the calendar screen must display ONLY a high-quality, pristine photographic image of a scenic spot from Sylhet, Bangladesh (e.g., Ratargul, Jaflong, tea gardens, Lalakhal, Bichanakandi, etc.).
- **Strictly No Titles, Captions, or Text Overlays**: Completely and permanently keep all titles, descriptions, location tags, and text overlays (such as 'জাফলং জিরো পয়েন্ট' or descriptive sentences) removed from all monthly image banner areas.
- **Full-Bleed Photographic Aesthetic**: The monthly banner must remain a full-bleed, clean, text-free scenic photograph across all 12 months dynamically. Month headers, year info, and navigation controls must always sit neatly below the banner image in the calendar body.

## 2. Member List Rules
- **Ascending / Seniority Sorting**: Members must always be displayed in ascending order by their serial number / registration order (oldest first).
- **No Joining Date**: The joining date field ('যোগদান: [তারিখ]') must remain removed from member cards.
